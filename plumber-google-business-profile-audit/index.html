12 checks before you pay for ongoing local SEO.

A useful Google Business Profile audit starts with factual customer-facing gaps—not keyword stuffing or ranking guarantees. Check these items against what a customer sees today.

Profile accuracy and relevance

  • 1. Real-world business nameDoes the profile use the name customers actually see, without added service or city keywords?
  • 2. Primary categoryDoes the main category reflect the core service, with secondary categories limited to services genuinely offered?
  • 3. Services and service areaAre priority jobs and the real coverage area clear, current, and consistent with the website?
  • 4. Hours and emergency claimsDo regular, holiday, and 24/7 claims agree everywhere a customer might check?

Trust and conversion

  • 5. Review-response gapsAre recent positive and critical reviews answered calmly, specifically, and without disclosing customer details?
  • 6. Photo evidenceDo recent, authentic photos show the team, vehicles, work, and service area more clearly than stock imagery?
  • 7. Call and booking pathCan a mobile visitor reach the right phone, quote form, or booking step without hunting?
  • 8. Proof near the actionAre licenses, guarantees, service boundaries, and credible testimonials placed beside the decision point?

Website and competitor alignment

  • 9. NAP consistencyDo the business name, address or service-area model, and phone agree across the profile, site, and major directories?
  • 10. Landing-page matchDoes the linked page clearly confirm the service and location promised by the profile?
  • 11. Broken trust cuesAre there conflicting hours, old closure notices, placeholder copy, dead links, or ambiguous locations?
  • 12. Three-neighbor comparisonWhat do three nearby competitors make easier to understand: service scope, availability, proof, or next action?
